DOT HAZMAT Course Options

Cover every required training category for hazmat employees under 49 CFR Part 172, Subpart H.

DOT HAZMAT General Awareness

Introductory training on recognizing and handling hazardous materials in the transportation chain.

Required Annually*

DOT HAZMAT Function-Specific

Job-specific training tailored to an employee's exact hazmat duties, such as packaging or documentation.

Required Annually*

DOT HAZMAT Safety Training

Covers emergency response information, safe handling practices, and measures to prevent hazmat incidents.

Required Annually*

DOT HAZMAT Security Awareness

Training on recognizing and responding to security risks associated with hazardous materials transport.

Required Annually*

3-Year DOT HAZMAT Recertification

Combined recertification training covering general awareness, function-specific, safety, and security topics.

Every 3 Years

*Recurring training intervals vary by requirement — courses are also available bundled as a 3-year recertification package.
