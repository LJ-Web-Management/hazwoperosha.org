About Us

Accredited Safety Training, Built to Be Shared

HAZWOPER OSHA Training delivers online HAZWOPER, OSHA, DOT, and workplace safety courses — and now, through the Training Partner Program, helps other businesses put that same training in front of their own customers.

Our Mission

Making Compliance Training Accessible

Our goal is to make accredited safety training simple to access, easy to understand, and available to every workforce that needs it — whether they come to us directly or through one of our Training Partners.

Every course in our library is built around OSHA, DOT, and industry compliance requirements, and offered in both English and Spanish so language is never a barrier to safety.

Why the Training Partner Program exists: Many businesses — associations, staffing agencies, distributors, and consultants — already have the audience that needs safety training. Rather than asking them to build a training platform from scratch, we give them a ready-made, accredited storefront instead.
