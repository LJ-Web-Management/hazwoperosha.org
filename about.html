About Us

FMCSA ELDT Theory Training, Built to Be Shared

HAZWOPER OSHA Training delivers online FMCSA ELDT theory training for entry-level commercial drivers, and, through the ELDT Partner Program, helps other businesses put that same training in front of their own customers, with every sale split 50/50.

Our Mission

Making ELDT Compliance Training Accessible

Our goal is to make accredited ELDT theory training simple to access, easy to understand, and available to every driver who needs it, whether they come to us directly or through one of our Training Partners.

Every course in our library is built around FMCSA ELDT theory requirements, and offered in both English and Spanish so language is never a barrier to safety.

Why the ELDT Partner Program exists: Many businesses, including associations, staffing agencies, distributors, and consultants, already have the audience that needs ELDT theory training. Rather than asking them to build a training platform from scratch, we give them a ready-made, accredited storefront instead, and split every sale with them evenly.

Who We Are

Part of HAZWOPER OSHA Training

The ELDT Partner Program is powered by HAZWOPER OSHA Training, a team of safety professionals delivering accredited compliance training nationwide.

We're a team of dedicated safety professionals committed to creating safer workplaces and preserving lives. Beyond ELDT, HAZWOPER OSHA Training specializes in HAZWOPER, RCRA, OSHA, DOT, HAZMAT, MSHA, and NFPA 70E training, delivered through the same EHS/LMS systems, SCORM-compliant courses, and accreditation services behind every ELDT Partner Program landing page.

Our mission: training that inspectors select for their clients, clients recommend to colleagues, teachers prefer for their students, employees are proud of, and investors seek for long-term returns.

One accredited provider, every course. Because your ELDT Partner Program landing page runs on the same infrastructure as HAZWOPER OSHA Training's full course catalog, your customers get the same accreditation, support, and quality behind every certificate, not a stripped-down version built just for partners.
