Employer Toolkit

These are reference templates, not fillable forms. Use them to build your own tracking sheet, or print each checklist for your compliance file.

📊

HAZWOPER Training Matrix

Map every role on your site to the training track it commonly requires, so nothing falls through the cracks.

  • List each role and its HAZWOPER category (e, p, or q)
  • Record initial training hours and completion date
  • Note supervisor or incident command add-ons
  • Flag next refresher due date

Sample matrix layout:

RoleCategoryInitial TrackAdd-OnRefresher Cycle
Cleanup Technician1910.120(e)40-Hour—Annual, 8-hr
Occasional Site Visitor1910.120(e)24-Hour—Annual, 8-hr
TSDF Operator1910.120(p)24-Hour TSDF—Annual, 8-hr TSDF
Site Supervisor1910.120(e)(4)40-Hour+8-Hr SupervisorAnnual, supervisor
Emergency Responder1910.120(q)24-Hour Emergency+Incident CommandAnnual

Annual Refresher Tracker

A simple way to track who is due for their 8-hour annual refresher, and when.

  • Employee name and current certification track
  • Date of last training (initial or refresher)
  • Refresher due date (typically 12 months later)
  • Status: current, due soon, or lapsed

Suggested columns for your tracker:

EmployeeTrackLast TrainedRefresher DueStatus
—40-Hour—+12 months—
—24-Hour TSDF—+12 months—
—Supervisor—+12 months—

Tip: flag anyone within 60 days of their due date as "due soon" so training can be scheduled before certification lapses.

New Employee Safety Onboarding Checklist

A starting checklist for bringing a new hire onto a HAZWOPER-covered site or role.

  • Confirm which HAZWOPER category applies to the role
  • Schedule initial training before unsupervised work begins
  • Assign supervised field experience days
  • Issue and document required PPE
  • Review site-specific hazards and emergency procedures

Site-Specific Training Checklist

General HAZWOPER training doesn't cover conditions unique to your site — this checklist helps close that gap.

  • Site layout, access points, and control zones
  • Site-specific hazards and monitoring locations
  • Communication protocol and chain of command
  • Decontamination station location and procedure

Emergency Response Readiness Checklist

A quick self-check for whether your site's emergency response plan and training are actually current.

  • Written emergency response plan on file and current
  • Response-level training documented for all responders
  • Incident command structure defined and trained
  • Drills conducted and logged on a regular schedule

Full readiness checklist:

ItemStatus
Written emergency response plan reviewed this year☐
All responders trained to their designated response level☐
Incident commander(s) identified and trained☐
Mutual aid / local emergency services contacted and briefed☐
PPE and response equipment inspected and stocked☐
Tabletop or live drill conducted in the last 12 months☐
Lessons from last drill or incident documented☐

These templates are a starting point, not a compliance guarantee. Your written HAZWOPER program should be reviewed against your actual site conditions and current OSHA requirements, ideally with input from a qualified safety professional.
