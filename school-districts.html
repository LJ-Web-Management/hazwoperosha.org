What's Included

One Seat. Both Required ELDT Theory Courses.

Every seat on your district's landing page covers the complete FMCSA ELDT theory curriculum a school bus driver candidate needs, bundled together at one per-seat price.

Class B CDL (ELDT) Theory

The core FMCSA entry-level driver theory training required before a candidate can test for a Class B commercial driver's license.

School Bus (S) Endorsement Theory

The additional FMCSA ELDT theory module required specifically for the School Bus (S) endorsement your district's routes depend on.

Certificates & Records Included

Each driver receives a printable certificate on completion, with records retained to support your district's compliance files.

Please note: This is ELDT theory training only, delivered entirely online. It does not replace the behind-the-wheel training and skills testing your district still coordinates separately for each driver.

How Volume Pricing Works

Bulk pricing is based on how many seats your district purchases across a calendar month, tracked automatically as you buy.

  • Purchase seats anytime at your district's current per-seat rate.
  • At the end of each month, we review total seats purchased.
  • If you've crossed into a new tier, email our team and we'll apply the lower rate going forward.

Crossed a threshold? Email sales@hazwoper-osha.com at the end of the month with your district name and seat count. Our team will confirm your new tier and adjust your per-seat pricing accordingly.

Frequently Asked Questions

School District ELDT FAQ

Do we purchase seats directly on this page?
No. This page shows your district's pricing and what's included, but there's no checkout here. Apply to the ELDT Partner Program, and we'll build a dedicated, branded landing page for your district, just like our other Training Partners, where you can purchase seats directly.
Does this cover the behind-the-wheel portion of ELDT?
No. This program covers the FMCSA ELDT theory curriculum for the Class B CDL and School Bus (S) Endorsement only. Your district still coordinates behind-the-wheel training and skills testing separately for each driver.
How does volume pricing work?
You purchase seats at your district's current per-seat rate at any time. At the end of each month, if your total seats purchased crossed into a new pricing tier, email sales@hazwoper-osha.com and our team will adjust your per-seat rate going forward.
Can we use a SCORM package instead of hosted seats?
Yes. If your district already runs an LMS, we can provide a SCORM-compliant package of the Class B CDL and School Bus (S) Endorsement theory courses so completions and records stay in your own system. Let us know when you apply, and we'll set your landing page up accordingly.
Is training available in Spanish?
Yes. Every course includes a built-in English/Spanish language toggle, so drivers can complete training in the language they prefer.
How do drivers receive their certificates?
Each driver receives a printable certificate immediately upon completing their course, with records retained to support your district's compliance files.
Can we pay by purchase order or invoice?
Contact our team to discuss payment options for district purchases, including purchase orders and invoicing for larger seat orders.
