# Hazmat Practice Quizzes

48 questions across six quizzes, each scored instantly with an explanation for every answer.

- [Safety Data Sheet (SDS) and GHS Label Quiz](https://www.hazwoperosha.org/quizzes/sds-and-ghs-quiz/) · Free quiz · 8 questions - Free safety data sheet quiz with answers: the 16 SDS sections, GHS pictograms, signal words, where to find PPE, exposure limits, flash point and first aid.
- [DOT Emergency Response Guidebook (ERG) Quiz](https://www.hazwoperosha.org/quizzes/erg-quiz/) · Free quiz · 8 questions - Free Emergency Response Guidebook quiz with answers: yellow, blue, orange and green pages, highlighted materials, guide numbers, hazard classes and first actions at a hazmat incident.
- [Toxicology Basics Quiz for Hazmat Workers](https://www.hazwoperosha.org/quizzes/toxicology-quiz/) · Free quiz · 8 questions - Free toxicology quiz with answers: dose-response, LD50, routes of entry, local vs systemic effects, target organs, sensitizers, asphyxiants and synergistic effects.
- [Respirator Selection and Fit Testing Quiz](https://www.hazwoperosha.org/quizzes/respirator-quiz/) · Free quiz · 8 questions - Free respirator quiz with answers: assigned protection factors, maximum use concentration, APR vs SCBA, cartridge colors, fit testing frequency and user seal checks under OSHA 1910.134.
- [Heat Stress in Protective Clothing Quiz](https://www.hazwoperosha.org/quizzes/heat-stress-quiz/) · Free quiz · 8 questions - Free heat stress quiz with answers: heat exhaustion vs heat stroke, first aid, acclimatization, hydration, work-rest cycles and heart-rate and temperature monitoring in chemical protective clothing.
- [Hazmat Field Safety Quiz: Drums, Confined Spaces and Spills](https://www.hazwoperosha.org/quizzes/hazmat-field-safety-quiz/) · Free quiz · 8 questions - Free hazmat field safety quiz with answers: drum handling under 1910.120(j), permit-required confined spaces, atmospheric testing order, incidental vs emergency spills and reporting.

## Study guides

- [How to Read a Safety Data Sheet (SDS): All 16 Sections Explained](https://www.hazwoperosha.org/guides/how-to-read-a-safety-data-sheet/) · Lesson · 3 min read - A section-by-section lesson on reading a GHS safety data sheet: hazard identification, pictograms, signal words, first aid, PPE, exposure limits, stability and spill response.
- [How to Use the DOT Emergency Response Guidebook (ERG)](https://www.hazwoperosha.org/guides/using-the-dot-emergency-response-guidebook/) · Lesson · 2 min read - Lesson on the DOT Emergency Response Guidebook: the yellow, blue, orange and green sections, UN/NA numbers, placards, highlighted materials and isolation distances.
- [Toxicology Basics for Hazmat Workers: Dose, Routes and Health Effects](https://www.hazwoperosha.org/guides/toxicology-basics-for-hazmat-workers/) · Lesson · 2 min read - Plain-English toxicology for hazardous waste and hazmat workers: dose-response, LD50 and LC50, local and systemic effects, target organs, carcinogens, sensitizers and synergy.
- [Respirator Selection and Fit Testing for HAZWOPER Work](https://www.hazwoperosha.org/guides/respirator-selection-and-fit-testing/) · Lesson · 2 min read - How to choose a respirator under OSHA 1910.134: assigned protection factors, maximum use concentration, cartridges and filters, fit testing, seal checks and change schedules.
- [Heat Stress in Chemical Protective Clothing: Signs, Prevention and Monitoring](https://www.hazwoperosha.org/guides/heat-stress-in-chemical-protective-clothing/) · Lesson · 3 min read - Why chemical protective clothing causes heat stress, the signs of heat exhaustion and heat stroke, work-rest cycles, hydration, acclimatization and physiological monitoring.
- [Drum and Container Handling at Hazardous Waste Sites](https://www.hazwoperosha.org/guides/drum-and-container-handling/) · Lesson · 2 min read - How HAZWOPER 1910.120(j) governs handling drums and containers: inspection, unlabeled drums, bulging and leaking drums, overpacks, opening, sampling, staging and buried drums.
