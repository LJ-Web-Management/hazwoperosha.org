# About HazwoperOSHA.org

HazwoperOSHA.org is a free HAZWOPER learning center. We publish plain-English lessons and practice quizzes on the hazmat skills behind HAZWOPER training, for new environmental technicians, emergency responders, students and experienced workers reviewing before a refresher, and offer certified online OSHA HAZWOPER courses for learners who need a certificate.

## Who runs HazwoperOSHA.org

HazwoperOSHA.org is operated by HAZWOPER OSHA Training LLC, a limited liability company registered in California with its office in Los Angeles. The same company runs [hazwoper-osha.com](https://hazwoper-osha.com/), the learning platform where the courses on this site are delivered, so a learner's login, progress, certificate and wallet card are all in one account.

## What we offer

- 42 online courses: OSHA HAZWOPER 40 hour, 24 hour and 8 hour refresher, supervisor, incident command, first responder awareness, industry-specific 40 hour, Cal/OSHA, Spanish, RCRA hazardous waste and TSDF training.
- 10 free lessons and 6 practice quizzes with answers, free to read with no sign-up.
- One cart and one checkout for a single learner or a whole team, with phone and email help for group orders and purchase orders.

## How the training works

Courses are 100% online and self-paced. Learners study on any device, progress saves automatically, and a certificate of completion and wallet card are available as soon as the final exam is passed. Each course page lists the regulation it is written to, such as OSHA 29 CFR 1910.120, 29 CFR 1926.65, Cal/OSHA 8 CCR 5192 or EPA RCRA. Online training covers the classroom portion of HAZWOPER; employers remain responsible for site-specific training and any supervised field experience OSHA requires.

## How we write our lessons

Our lessons summarize federal OSHA, Cal/OSHA, EPA and DOT rules in plain English for training and review. They are not legal advice, so always check the current regulation text and your site safety and health plan.
