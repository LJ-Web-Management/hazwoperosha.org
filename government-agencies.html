Frequently Asked Questions

Government Agency ELDT FAQ

Do we purchase seats directly on this page?
No. This page shows your agency's pricing and what's included, but there's no checkout here. Apply to the ELDT Partner Program, and we'll build a dedicated, branded landing page for your agency, just like our other Training Partners, where you can purchase seats directly.
Which CDL classes and endorsements are included?
Every seat draws from the complete FMCSA ELDT theory course library: Class A, Class B, the Class B to Class A upgrade, and the HAZMAT (H), Hazmat and Tank Vehicle (X), Tanker (N), Passenger (P), and School Bus (S) endorsements. Departments can train employees on only the courses their vehicles require.
Does this cover the behind-the-wheel portion of ELDT?
No. This program covers the FMCSA ELDT theory curriculum only. Your agency still coordinates behind-the-wheel training and skills testing separately for each driver.
How does volume pricing work?
You purchase seats at your agency's current per-seat rate at any time. At the end of each month, if your total seats purchased crossed into a new pricing tier, email sales@hazwoper-osha.com and our team will adjust your per-seat rate going forward.
Can we use a SCORM package instead of hosted seats?
Yes. If your agency already runs an LMS, we can provide a SCORM-compliant package of any course in the ELDT theory library so completions and records stay in your own system. Let us know when you apply, and we'll set your landing page up accordingly.
Can multiple departments purchase and track seats separately?
Yes. Departments can purchase seats for the specific courses their drivers need, and seat totals across your agency are combined when determining your volume pricing tier.
Is training available in Spanish?
Yes. Every course includes a built-in English/Spanish language toggle, so employees can complete training in the language they prefer.
Can we pay by purchase order or invoice?
Yes. Contact our team to discuss payment options for agency purchases, including purchase orders, invoicing, and any vendor documentation your procurement process requires.
