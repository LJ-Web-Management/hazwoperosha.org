## Before you get in touch

- Not sure which course you need? Start with the [frequently asked questions](https://www.hazwoperosha.org/#faq) or the [lessons](https://www.hazwoperosha.org/guides/).
- Buying for a team? Add every course to the [cart](https://www.hazwoperosha.org/checkout.html), set the number of seats for each and check out once, or email us for a quote.
- Already enrolled? Learner logins are emailed once your order is confirmed. Sign in at [hazwoper-osha.com](https://hazwoper-osha.com/) to continue your course.

Include your name, your company if you are ordering for one, the courses and the number of learners, so we can answer in one reply.
