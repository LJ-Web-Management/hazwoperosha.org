What Is the Training Partner Program

Your Storefront. Our Training Library.

The Training Partner Program gives any business, including associations, staffing agencies, contractors, distributors, or consultants, a dedicated landing page where their audience can purchase accredited safety training directly from HAZWOPER OSHA Training's course library.

A Dedicated Landing Page

Every partner receives their own landing page showcasing our full training library, ready for their customers to browse and enroll.

No Cost to Get Started

Use our standard branding at no charge, or invest a one-time $750 build fee for a page that looks and feels fully like your own.

Zero Operational Burden

HAZWOPER OSHA Training remains the seller of record. We manage checkout, hosting, certificates, and customer support end to end.

Revenue & Ownership

Simple, Transparent Revenue Structure

HAZWOPER OSHA Training is the accredited provider and seller of record for every course sold through a partner landing page. All course revenue is collected and retained by HAZWOPER OSHA Training.

Partners can direct their own customers to purchase training themselves, or place orders on their customers' behalf. Either way, checkout, certificates, and compliance recordkeeping are handled entirely by our team.

Learn How Revenue Works

Good to know: Because HAZWOPER OSHA Training remains the merchant and training provider of record, partners take on no compliance liability, payment processing, or course-content responsibility. The landing page is simply your branded doorway into our library.
