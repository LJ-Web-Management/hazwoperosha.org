What Is the ELDT Partner Program

Your Storefront. Our ELDT Theory Training.

The ELDT Partner Program gives any business, including associations, staffing agencies, contractors, distributors, or consultants, a dedicated landing page where their audience can purchase ELDT theory training, listed on the FMCSA Training Provider Registry, directly from HAZWOPER OSHA Training.

A Dedicated Landing Page

Every partner receives their own landing page showcasing our full ELDT theory course library, ready for their customers to browse and enroll.

No Cost to Get Started

Every partner launches on HAZWOPER OSHA Training's branding at no charge. There's no setup fee, no build fee, and no recurring platform cost.

50/50 Revenue Split

Revenue & Ownership

Simple, Transparent Revenue Structure

HAZWOPER OSHA Training is the accredited provider and seller of record for every course sold through a partner landing page. Course revenue is split 50/50 between HAZWOPER OSHA Training and the partner.

Partners can direct their own customers to purchase training themselves, or place orders on their customers' behalf. Either way, checkout, certificates, and compliance recordkeeping are handled entirely by our team.

Good to know: Because HAZWOPER OSHA Training remains the merchant and training provider of record, partners take on no compliance liability, payment processing, or course-content responsibility.
