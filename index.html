About the learning center

HazwoperOSHA.org is for anyone learning to work safely around hazardous materials: new environmental technicians, emergency responders, students, and experienced workers brushing up before a refresher.

Our lessons summarize the OSHA, EPA and DOT rules and the NIOSH guidance that shape hazmat work, in plain English. They're written for training and review, so always follow your site safety and health plan and the current regulation text.

When you need a certificate

  • Self-paced courses that meet OSHA 29 CFR 1910.120 training requirements
  • Certificate and wallet card as soon as you pass
  • Federal OSHA, Cal/OSHA and state-plan versions
  • 40-hour course available in Spanish

Learning Center FAQ

Are the lessons and quizzes on HazwoperOSHA.org free?

Yes. Every lesson and practice quiz on this site is free to read and take, with no account or sign-up.

Do the free lessons count as OSHA HAZWOPER training?

No. The lessons help you learn and review, but OSHA training requirements are met by completing a full course with a final exam and certificate, plus any supervised field experience your level requires.

Where should a beginner start?

Start with the lessons on reading a safety data sheet and toxicology basics, take the matching quizzes, then enroll in the 40 hour HAZWOPER course if you will work on cleanup sites.

What are the lessons based on?

They summarize OSHA standards (including 29 CFR 1910.120, 1910.134, 1910.146 and 1910.1200), EPA and DOT rules, the DOT Emergency Response Guidebook and NIOSH guidance. They are written for training purposes and are not legal advice.

Can supervisors and trainers use these lessons with a crew?

Yes. Link your crew to any lesson or quiz for a toolbox talk or pre-course review.

How should I prepare for the final exam?

Study in focused blocks, take notes per module and test yourself with the practice quizzes. The lesson on studying for the HAZWOPER exam includes a table of the key numbers to know.

What do I get when I finish a certificate course?

A printable certificate of completion and a wallet card, available as soon as you pass the final exam.

Are courses available in Spanish?

Yes. The OSHA 40-Hour HAZWOPER course has a full Spanish-language version. Questions? Call +1-866-429-6742 or email sales@hazwoper-osha.com.
