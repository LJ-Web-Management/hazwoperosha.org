Which best describes you or your crew?

Commonly Applies

40-Hour HAZWOPER Training

Workers who regularly perform hazardous substance removal, containment, or cleanup at a site covered by 29 CFR 1910.120(e) commonly need the 40-hour HAZWOPER course, plus at least 3 days of supervised field experience before working unsupervised.

View 40-Hour Course Learn More

Some states run their own OSHA-approved plan with added requirements — check our State OSHA Guide, including Cal/OSHA specifics.

Commonly Applies

Emergency Response Training (24-Hour + Incident Command)

Emergency responders are trained by response level under 1910.120(q), from Awareness through Incident Commander. Many teams start with 24-hour HAZWOPER emergency response training and add 8-hour Incident Command training for personnel directing the response.

View 24-Hour Course Incident Command Training

The exact response level and hours needed depend on your specific duties under your facility's emergency response plan.

Commonly Applies

24-Hour HAZWOPER — TSD Operations

Employees at a permitted hazardous waste Treatment, Storage, or Disposal Facility (TSDF) are covered by 1910.120(p) and commonly need 24 hours of initial training specific to TSDF operations, with an 8-hour annual refresher afterward.

View TSDF Course Learn More

TSDF training requirements are distinct from the cleanup-site (e) requirements — confirm which subsection covers your facility.

Commonly Applies

24-Hour HAZWOPER Training

Workers who are on a hazardous waste site occasionally for a specific, limited task, and who are unlikely to exceed permissible exposure limits, commonly qualify for the reduced 24-hour track instead of the full 40-hour course.

View 24-Hour Course Learn More

Whether the 24-hour track applies depends on actual exposure potential, not just job title — your employer's site safety plan should confirm this.

Commonly Applies

8-Hour HAZWOPER Supervisor Training

Site supervisors and managers who oversee HAZWOPER operations commonly need 8 hours of additional supervisor-specific training covering their oversight and safety-program responsibilities, on top of the 40-hour (or 24-hour) worker training.

View Supervisor Course Learn More

Supervisor training builds on, and doesn't replace, the underlying 40-hour or 24-hour worker certification.

Commonly Applies

8-Hour Annual Refresher

Workers already HAZWOPER-certified commonly need an 8-hour refresher each year to keep their certification current. If your prior training was TSDF-specific or Supervisor-level, take the matching refresher track.

View Refresher Course Learn More

Also need a TSDF or Supervisor refresher specifically? See TSDF refresher or Supervisor refresher.
