Program Terms

Minimum Performance & Termination Policy

A simple performance standard keeps the ELDT Partner Program sustainable for everyone involved.

Minimum sales threshold: Partner landing pages are expected to generate at least $50 in course sales per month, starting 60 days after the page first goes live.

If a partner page falls below this threshold after that 60-day grace period, HAZWOPER OSHA Training reserves the right, at its own discretion, to terminate the ELDT Partner Program agreement for that page, with at least 1 month written notice. This is an option we may choose to exercise. It is not an automatic or required action, and we may continue supporting a page below this threshold if we choose to.

This policy applies only to the minimum sales threshold above. It does not limit either party's other rights, and specific partnership terms may be adjusted in a signed agreement between HAZWOPER OSHA Training and the partner.

Frequently Asked Questions

ELDT Partner Program FAQ

Is there a cost to join the ELDT Partner Program?
No. There is no setup fee and no cost to join. Every partner launches on HAZWOPER OSHA Training's standard branding.
How is revenue split?
HAZWOPER OSHA Training is the accredited training provider and seller of record, and handles checkout, certificates, recordkeeping, and support. Course revenue is split 50/50 between HAZWOPER OSHA Training and the partner.
Can my customers purchase courses directly, or do I have to buy for them?
Both options are available. Your customers can purchase courses themselves directly through your landing page, or you can purchase training on their behalf, whichever workflow fits your business best.
Is training available in Spanish?
Yes. Every course includes a built-in English/Spanish language toggle, so learners can complete training in the language they prefer.
How long does it take to launch a landing page?
Since every page uses our standard branding, there's no custom design work required, so pages can typically launch quickly after your application is approved.
Do I need to handle customer support or compliance recordkeeping?
No. HAZWOPER OSHA Training handles course delivery, checkout, certificates, recordkeeping, and customer support for every purchase made through a partner landing page.
What happens if my page isn't generating sales?
Partner pages are expected to generate at least $50 in course sales per month, starting 60 days after launch. If a page falls below that after the 60-day grace period, we reserve the option, but are not required, to terminate the agreement, with at least 1 month written notice. See Program Terms above for full details.
