Program Terms

Minimum Performance & Termination Policy

A simple performance standard keeps the Training Partner Program sustainable for everyone involved.

Minimum sales threshold: Partner landing pages are expected to generate at least $100 in course sales per month.

If a partner page falls below this threshold, HAZWOPER OSHA Training reserves the right, at its own discretion, to terminate the Training Partner Program agreement for that page. This is an option we may choose to exercise. It is not an automatic or required action, and we may continue supporting a page below this threshold if we choose to.

Termination notice: If HAZWOPER OSHA Training elects to terminate under this policy, notice will be given in writing as follows:

Branding OptionMinimum Notice Before Termination
HAZWOPER OSHA Branding (no setup fee)1 month written notice
Custom Branding ($750 one-time build fee)1 year written notice

This policy applies only to the minimum sales threshold above. It does not limit either party's other rights, and specific partnership terms may be adjusted in a signed agreement between HAZWOPER OSHA Training and the partner.

Frequently Asked Questions

Training Partner Program FAQ

Is there a cost to join the Training Partner Program?
There is no setup fee if you use HAZWOPER OSHA Training's standard branding. If you'd like a fully custom-branded landing page built around your own logo and colors, there is a one-time $750 build fee.
Who receives the revenue from course purchases?
HAZWOPER OSHA Training is the accredited training provider and seller of record, so all course revenue is collected and retained by HAZWOPER OSHA Training regardless of which branding option a partner chooses.
Can my customers purchase courses directly, or do I have to buy for them?
Both options are available. Your customers can purchase courses themselves directly through your landing page, or you can purchase training on their behalf, whichever workflow fits your business best.
What courses can I offer on my landing page?
Your page draws from our complete training library, including HAZWOPER, OSHA 10 & 30-Hour, DOT HAZMAT, ELDT, RCRA, Forklift Certification, First Aid/CPR/AED, and Bloodborne Pathogens courses.
Is training available in Spanish?
Yes. Every course includes a built-in English/Spanish language toggle, so learners can complete training in the language they prefer.
How long does it take to launch a landing page?
Pages using our standard branding can typically launch the fastest since no custom design work is required. Custom-branded pages take a bit longer to allow time for design and development.
Do I need to handle customer support or compliance recordkeeping?
No. HAZWOPER OSHA Training handles course delivery, checkout, certificates, recordkeeping, and customer support for every purchase made through a partner landing page.
What happens if my page isn't generating sales?
Partner pages are expected to generate at least $100 in course sales per month. If a page falls below that, we reserve the option, but are not required, to terminate the agreement, with 1 month notice for HAZWOPER OSHA branded pages or 1 year notice for custom-branded pages. See Program Terms above for full details.
